1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Campus Connect may collect the following information.

Account information

  • email address;
  • password credentials in protected account-storage form;
  • name;
  • phone number, if you provide one;
  • account role and account-related settings;
  • account creation and related timestamps.

Marketplace information

  • listing and wanted-post titles and descriptions;
  • prices or budgets;
  • categories;
  • listing/wanted-post ownership information;
  • marketplace transaction information;
  • images you choose to upload;
  • image file metadata needed to process and store uploaded images;
  • information needed to connect buyers and sellers.

Marketplace content may be visible to other users where the feature is designed to make that content public.

Contact information

If you provide a phone number or WhatsApp number for marketplace or support-related functionality, we may use it to enable communication or contact between users and the service.

Payment information

For payments, donations, marketplace fees, or other paid services, the service may process payment amount, purpose, transaction identifiers, payment status and timestamps, provider references, and the phone number used to initiate an M-Pesa/mobile-money payment. An email address may also be processed where required by a payment provider.

Payments may be processed through M-Pesa and/or Paystack depending on which payment method is enabled. Campus Connect does not store your M-Pesa PIN.

AI and assistant interactions

If you use AI-powered features, we may process questions and prompts, files or documents you voluntarily upload, AI-generated responses, conversation or interaction history, account association information, and technical AI usage information such as model, token usage, processing duration, status, and cost.

Depending on the feature and configuration, requests may be processed through third-party AI or AI-support services, which can include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google/Gemini, Groq, PageIndex, and other configured providers.

WhatsApp functionality

If you use WhatsApp-related features, the service may process your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name where provided, message content, message identifiers, timestamps, message type, voice-note audio, voice-note transcriptions, and AI-generated draft responses. WhatsApp services are provided through Meta's WhatsApp platform.

Feedback and support

Feedback may include your account identifier, feedback category, feedback message, associated app screen, and submission timestamp.

Usage and technical information

The application and backend may process technical information necessary to authenticate requests, protect the service, enforce rate limits, diagnose errors, and maintain reliability. The current application does not intentionally use advertising SDKs or an advertising identifier for targeted advertising.

2. How we use information

  • create and manage accounts;
  • authenticate users and protect accounts;
  • provide marketplace functionality;
  • facilitate marketplace connections and transactions;
  • process payments and donations;
  • provide AI assistant and document-processing features;
  • provide WhatsApp-related functionality;
  • respond to feedback and support requests;
  • detect and prevent inappropriate marketplace content;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the service;
  • monitor service usage and AI processing costs;
  • comply with legal, security, and fraud-prevention requirements.

We do not sell personal information.

3. How information is shared

We may share or transmit information with service providers when necessary to provide a feature or operate the service. Depending on the feature, these may include hosting and database providers, cloud object-storage providers, payment providers such as M-Pesa and Paystack, Meta/WhatsApp, AI providers, transcription providers, and other technical service providers.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, to protect service security, investigate abuse or fraud, or protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

4. Marketplace visibility

Campus Connect is a marketplace and connection service. Information you deliberately include in a marketplace listing or wanted post may be displayed to other authenticated users so they can discover and interact with the post. Do not publish information you do not want other marketplace users to see.

5. Image storage

Marketplace images you upload are processed and stored by the service's configured image-storage system. Images may be stored in cloud object storage when cloud storage is enabled. Access to stored images may use time-limited URLs.

Under the current marketplace configuration, sold listings are scheduled for deletion after a retention period of seven days.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain transaction or operational records. Retention periods vary by data type and feature.

Marketplace sold listings are currently scheduled for automatic deletion after seven days. Query history can be deleted through the available history-management functionality. Some information may need to be retained longer for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction purposes.

7. Account deletion

If you request deletion of your Campus Connect account, we will delete or de-identify account-associated information that we are not required or permitted to retain.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or completed financial transactions.

Developer action required before production submission: Google Play requires account deletion to be available both inside the app and through an external web-based deletion resource. The implementation should be completed before production review.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including authenticated access controls, protected authentication mechanisms, encrypted connections where supported by the service, and controlled access to stored information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Campus Connect is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly use the service to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or Google Play requirements.

10. Third-party services

Some Campus Connect features depend on third-party services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Examples include Meta/WhatsApp, payment providers, AI providers, transcription providers, and cloud infrastructure or object-storage providers.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of information associated with your account and to ask questions about how your information is handled. Requests can be made using the privacy contact below.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices, legal obligations, or third-party integrations change. The updated version will be posted at the public Privacy Policy URL together with the date of the latest update.
