Campus Connect Account Deletion

Campus Connect is operated by YOUR FULL NAME. This page explains how you can request deletion of your Campus Connect account and associated data.

Delete your account in the app

If you can access Campus Connect, you can permanently delete your account directly from the app:

Account → Delete my account

The deletion process removes your account and associated application data eligible for deletion.

Request deletion if you cannot access the app

If you cannot access your Campus Connect account, you can request account deletion by contacting us using the email address below.

Request account deletion

When submitting a request, include the email address associated with your Campus Connect account so that we can identify the account and process the request.

What will be deleted

When an account deletion request is successfully processed, we delete data associated with the account that is eligible for deletion. This may include:

  • account information;
  • marketplace listings and wanted posts;
  • uploaded marketplace images;
  • feedback associated with the account;
  • query history;
  • AI usage information associated with the account;
  • WhatsApp-related application data associated with the account;
  • payment and transaction information that is eligible for deletion; and
  • other application data associated with the account that is eligible for deletion.

Information that may be retained

Some information may be retained where necessary or permitted for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting, completed financial transactions, or other legitimate legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it must be retained.
